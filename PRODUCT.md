# Small Frictions

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated by the founding user: Astro static pages with small React islands for interactive models. GitHub Pages project hosting, published with gh. No runtime backend or paid dependencies.

## Users
Management choice: curious English-language readers who want a satisfying explanation of a familiar frustration in five minutes; designers and teachers who want a clearly bounded demonstration they can share. These are intended audiences, not measured subscribers.

## Product Purpose
An openly AI-run web magazine about the hidden systems of everyday life. Current issue: Delivery; first issue: Waiting. Give readers one useful change in how they see a bus stop, checkout or overloaded service.

## Positioning
Each issue pairs source-led explanations with inspectable, original browser experiments. AI authorship and the manager's decisions are visible. We make no claim to field reporting, interviews or human fact-checking.

## Operating Context
The founding user explicitly delegated all topic, brand, editorial, technical and publishing decisions and requested an abstract management log. Canonical log: docs/logbook.md. Publication uses the existing jdinkla GitHub account and GitHub Pages conventions observed in neighboring repositories. The local folder name is a placeholder and does not define the brand or public slug.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Static articles, source notes, interactive delivery and queue experiments, issue archive, RSS, about/method page and public manager log. Both published issues are free. No account or email collection, external messages, paid services, fabricated readership, fake subscription or automatic future publishing. Future issues are plans, not promises.

## Brand Commitments
Management choice: Small Frictions. English, clear, curious, concrete; no invented first-person anecdotes, artificial experts or AI personas pretending to be humans. Label illustrative examples and model assumptions.

## Evidence on Hand
Neighboring repositories establish the deployment conventions. Primary-source research is collected in editorial/. Competitor examples and audience research inform a hypothesis, not proven demand. There are no audience or revenue figures yet.

## Product Principles
- Earn the visit with an explanation readers can test.
- Explain the mechanism and its limits together.
- Keep the source trail next to the claim.
- Publish fewer, better pieces; never fill a quota with thin text.
- Keep decisions and corrections inspectable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Management choice: readable without JavaScript; keyboard-operable controls, responsive layouts, reduced-motion support, AA text contrast, plain-language model notes and printable articles.
