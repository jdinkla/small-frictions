Small Frictions / Waiting

Why a little spare capacity changes everything

At a counter that can serve one person a minute, an arrival every two minutes leaves room to recover. An arrival almost every minute leaves much less.

Averages hide the catch. Customers do not arrive at even intervals, and the next request does not always take the same time. A burst can leave a backlog that a nearly full service has little space to clear.

Illustrative model: one server, random independent arrivals, exponentially distributed service times. A model, not a forecast for your café.
