# Publishing hypothesis

**Audience job:** “Explain the thing I keep noticing, and let me try it.”
**Offer:** one coherent issue, six evidence-led reads and one inspectable lab; no assumed reporting or personal expertise.
**Acquisition:** specific question-led titles, indexable static article pages, small shareable experiments, RSS and issue-level return visits. No outreach or promotion has been sent. Search visibility is a hypothesis; no keyword volume was invented.
**Retention:** future themed issues, navigable archive, working RSS. No fixed cadence until a second issue has been researched and quality checked.
**Economics:** no paid service or incremental hosting purchase in this launch; existing GitHub entitlement applies. Future sponsorship is conditional on real reader demand and clearly separated from editorial decisions. No subscription form until a real delivery service exists.
**Success to evaluate:** actual reader feedback, repeat visits if privacy-respecting measurement is later authorized, source corrections, whether readers use the lab. Current audience and revenue: unknown, not zero measurements.
**Risk:** AI authorship may deter readers; breadth can become shallow; diagrams may imply more certainty than evidence allows. Mitigation: explicit authorship, issue focus, concrete examples, named assumptions and source notes.

## Evidence consulted 2026-11-08
- [Reuters Institute, Generative AI and News Report 2025](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/generative-ai-and-news-report-2025-how-people-think-about-ais-role-journalism-and-society): survey evidence of caution about AI-produced news. It does not establish demand for Small Frictions.
- [The Pudding, About](https://pudding.cool/about/): an existing publisher’s description of the visual-essay format and evergreen editorial approach. Reference category, not endorsement or revenue proxy.
- [Nicky Case, Explorable Explanations](https://blog.ncase.me/explorable-explanations/): creator’s explanation of interaction as a way to understand systems. Design rationale, not conversion evidence.
